=============================================================
     THE BOSSWEBBER WEB DEVELOPMENT GUIDE
=============================================================
Understanding Our Process, Tech, and What to Expect.

Welcome to the Bosswebber guide! Building a website can feel overwhelming, but we've streamlined our process to keep you involved and stress-free at every stage.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: DISCOVERY & STRATEGY (Week 1)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Everything begins with a deep dive into your business.
We discuss your goals, review your provided checklist, and agree on the project scope. 
• Deliverable: A comprehensive Project Proposal & Timeline.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: UI/UX DESIGN (Week 1 - 2)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Before we write a single line of code, we design.
We create high-fidelity mockups of your future website. We focus heavily on modern aesthetics, mobile responsiveness, and intuitive navigation.
• Deliverable: A clickable design prototype for your approval.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3: DEVELOPMENT (Week 2 - 4)
-------------------------------------------------------------
This is where the magic happens. 
We leverage state-of-the-art technologies (React, Next.js, Vite) to build a lightning-fast, secure website. Unlike cheap templates, our code is completely custom, ensuring maximum performance and SEO capabilities.
• Deliverable: A private staging link where you can watch the site come to life.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4: TESTING & REVISIONS (Week 4)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Bosswebber packages include up to 3 rounds of free revisions! 
We test the site on different browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) and mobile devices. We rigorously test contact forms, payment gateways (including MTN MoMo), and load speeds.
• Deliverable: Finalized, polished website ready for deployment.

-------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 5: LAUNCH & HANDOVER (Final Step)
-------------------------------------------------------------
We migrate the site to your live domain. We handle all the SSL (security) configurations and backend setup. 
• Deliverable: A live website and a training session so you know how to update the content yourself!

-------------------------------------------------------------
POST-LAUNCH: SUPPORT
-------------------------------------------------------------
Depending on the plan you chose, Bosswebber provides 1 to 6 months of free technical support to handle any inevitable bugs or necessary platform updates.


WHY TECHNICAL PERFORMANCE MATTERS:
Your website is deployed using globally distributed Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) and optimized image formats. This means your customers will experience load times often under 1 second—drastically reducing bounce rates and improving Google Search rankings.
